# the scar that started dodo payments.

a payments company built from the revenue its founder could not collect.

december 16, 2025by shashank sn·7 min read

before dodo payments existed, ayush agarwal was trying to collect money from users of tournafest, his gaming platform.

the users were there, many of them outside india. their payment methods, taxes, and compliance requirements sat beyond what the connected gateways could handle, leaving willing customers and unreachable revenue.

that frustration became the starting point for dodo payments.

## a scar is stronger than a market gap

ayush had lived the problem from the founder’s side. rishabh goel had seen the infrastructure side through his work in payments. they started dodo payments in 2023 around a practical belief: a person building a digital product should be able to sell globally without learning the tax code of every customer’s country.

their official account of the company’s origin says tournafest had more than 100,000 users when the payment problem became impossible to ignore. in early 2025, dodo payments announced a $1.1 million pre-seed round.

> the company began with trapped revenue.

founder-problem fit gets discussed like an investor checklist. the useful version is simpler. a founder who has carried the problem remembers the sharp edges that research summaries smooth away.

## the name lowers the temperature

payment companies often reach for names that sound secure, global, and institutional. the result is a category full of interchangeable seriousness.

dodo is an extinct bird. it is approachable, easy to remember, and slightly odd. that energy fits the people the company speaks to: solo developers, indie hackers, and small software teams who want the financial machinery to feel less intimidating.

the name performs a job before the sales copy begins. it tells a tired founder that the conversation may be understandable.

## the product has to prove the language

dodo payments describes itself as a merchant of record. in practice, that means it becomes the legal seller and handles payment processing, sales-tax work, fraud, disputes, and billing responsibilities for the product maker.

the brand promise is reduced complexity. the dashboard, onboarding, documentation, pricing, and support all have to carry that same promise. one confusing workflow would say more than a clean homepage ever could.

many brand systems fail at this handoff. marketing owns the words while another team owns the proof, and customers experience every join.

## choose a smaller game you can define

dodo payments chose digital products and the builders behind them, giving the company a narrower game it could define.

that choice influences the product, content, partnerships, and vocabulary. the audience has a specific problem. the company can build the whole experience around it.

- 01start with the scar. lived frustration reveals details a broad market thesis misses.

- 02match the emotional state. an approachable name can lower the pressure of a difficult category.

- 03make the product carry the promise. simplicity has to survive contact with onboarding, billing, and support.

- 04define the customer narrowly. focus gives a small team decisions it can actually make.

## the part worth stealing

the lesson has little to do with payment infrastructure.

large competitors already own the obvious version of most categories. a smaller company needs a game shaped around its own closeness to the problem. dodo payments found that game in builders who wanted to sell globally and keep building.

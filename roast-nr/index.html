pain points
critical

ai voice drift

every ai tool outputs the same generic cadence. your readers can smell "delve", "leverage", and "in today's digital landscape" from a mile away. the problem is not the model. every model defaults to the average voice of the internet.

critical

no real-time signal

writers drift not because they do not know the rules. they get zero feedback in the moment of writing. by the time you notice the slop, it has already shipped. voice consistency is a feedback loop problem, not a documentation problem.

advisory

team inconsistency

three writers produce three different sounding outputs. brand voice fractures. onboarding new writers means re-explaining tone from scratch every single time. there is no centralized signal or shared standard.

advisory

static prompts degrade

system prompts fade over time. week 1 your voice match is strong. by week 8 it has dropped 19%. you re-prompt and it drifts again. hyv does the opposite. it learns from every accept or reject and compounds over time.

what they cannot solve

  • every model sounds different. claude is not gpt which is not gemini. your profile needs to be portable

a voice layer that compounds.

  • one profile across every model. claude, chatgpt, cursor, codex all use the same voice
  • zero api cost for scanning. ci integration blocks slop before merge
